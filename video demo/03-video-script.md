## Production notes
- Use a consistent cursor highlight or click-ripple effect so viewers can always tell what was clicked.
- Blur or replace any real buyer/seller PII (names, SSNs, emails, phone numbers) with sample data before recording.
- Keep each screen on-camera for at least 2–3 seconds before the next cut — don't rush past tables/forms.
- Background music, if used, should stay low under narration (~-20dB under voice).
